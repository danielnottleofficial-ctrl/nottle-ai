# Google Play Data safety answers — version 1.0

These answers describe the current NOTTLE AI Android app and connected service. Re-check them whenever analytics, advertising, payments or another SDK is added.

## Overview answers

- Does the app collect or share required user data types? **Yes — collects data.**
- Is any user data sold? **No.**
- Is data used for advertising or cross-app tracking? **No.**
- Is collected data encrypted in transit? **Yes.** The Android app uses HTTPS only and telephone provider connections are encrypted in transit.
- Can users request deletion? **Yes.** Users can delete their account in Settings and at `https://nottle-ai.onrender.com/delete-account`.
- Does the app allow account creation? **Yes.**
- Does the app provide an in-app account deletion path? **Yes — Settings > Delete account.**

NOTTLE AI uses OpenAI, Twilio and the hosting/email providers as service providers to operate the requested service. No data is shared for advertising, sold, or used for the provider's independent marketing. In the Play form, select **not shared** only while those providers remain contracted processors/service providers under Google's exclusion for service-provider transfers.

## Data types to declare

| Play data type | Collected | Shared | Required or optional | Purpose | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info — Name | Yes | No | Required for account | Account management; app functionality | Linked to account; retained until deletion |
| Personal info — Email address | Yes | No | Required for account | Authentication; support; password reset | Linked to account; retained until deletion |
| Personal info — Phone number | Yes | No | Optional for account owner; caller number is captured during a call | App functionality; lead follow-up | Linked to business call record; user can delete |
| Personal info — Other info | Yes | No | Optional | Business name, service area, hours, services and assistant instructions | Linked to account; user can edit/delete |

| Audio — Voice or sound recordings | Yes, processed ephemerally | No | Required only when a caller uses the AI phone service | Real-time AI call functionality and transcription | Audio is transmitted for real-time processing; NOTTLE AI does not store call audio |
| App info and performance — Diagnostics | Yes | No | Automatic | Security, reliability and troubleshooting | Limited service logs; not used for advertising |
| Device or other identifiers — Other identifiers | Yes | No | Automatic | Authentication, fraud prevention and security | Session token and browser/app user-agent; not an advertising ID |

## Data types not collected in version 1.0

- Precise or approximate device location
- Contacts or address book
- Photos, videos or files
- Calendar data
- Financial or payment information
- Health and fitness information
- Web browsing history
- Advertising ID

## Security and retention statement

- Passwords are stored as one-way password hashes.
- Login sessions are stored as hashes and expire automatically.
- Twilio webhook requests are signature-validated in production.
- Accounts are separated by business in the application database.
- Account and call data is retained while the account is active or until the user deletes it.
- Individual calls can be deleted. Full account deletion removes the account, business setup, sessions and stored call records from active storage.
- Infrastructure backup remnants, if present, expire under the relevant provider's backup schedule.
