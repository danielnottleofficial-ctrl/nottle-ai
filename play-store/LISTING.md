## Full description

Meet NOTTLE AI, the AI receptionist built for busy Australian small businesses.

When you are on a job, with a customer or away from the phone, NOTTLE AI can answer incoming calls, understand the enquiry and organise the details for follow-up.

YOUR BUSINESS, YOUR ASSISTANT

Set your business name, service area, opening hours, services, greeting and conversation style. Choose a voice and listening pace that suits the way your customers speak.

CALLS AND LEADS IN ONE PLACE

Review recent calls, caller details, clear enquiry summaries, complete transcripts and booking or quote requests from a secure dashboard.

NATURAL, PATIENT CONVERSATIONS

NOTTLE AI is designed to ask one question at a time, listen through natural pauses and let callers finish before responding. The assistant identifies itself as AI and advises callers that the conversation will be transcribed.

BUILT-IN PRIVACY CONTROLS

Export your information, delete individual call records or permanently delete your account and stored data from the app.

Key features:

- AI phone receptionist for customer enquiries
- Custom business profile, services, hours and greeting
- Choice of assistant voice and listening pace
- Call summaries, transcripts and lead details
- Booking and quote-request identification
- Secure account access and business separation
- Data export, call deletion and account deletion
- Australian English conversation style

NOTTLE AI is intended for routine business enquiries. Important details, availability and commitments should always be reviewed and confirmed by the business.

Phone-number activation and AI calling require an active NOTTLE AI service plan and supported telephony availability.
