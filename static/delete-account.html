Delete your account

Public deletion instructions for NOTTLE AI users.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open NOTTLE AI and sign in.
  2. Select Settings.
  3. Under Delete account, select Delete.
  4. Type DELETE, enter your password and confirm.

What is deleted

Your login profile, business setup, assistant instructions, active sessions, stored call records, transcripts and summaries are permanently removed from active application storage. The action cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in

Email us from the address registered to your account with the subject “Delete my NOTTLE AI account”. We may ask for additional information to verify account ownership before deletion.

Carrier and legal records

Telecommunications providers may retain limited billing, regulatory or fraud-prevention records under their own legal obligations. We may retain information only where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims.
