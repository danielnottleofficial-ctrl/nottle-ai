Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Last updated 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NOTTLE AI, operated by DN Property Projects in Western Australia (“we”, “us”), handles personal information when businesses and their callers use our AI receptionist service, website and Android app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password hash and account settings.
  • Business configuration: business name, services, operating area, hours, contact details and instructions given to the assistant.
  • Call and lead information: caller number, call time and duration, enquiry details, call transcript, AI-generated summary and booking intent. We do not store call audio in the current service.
  • Technical information: session information, device/browser details, security events and service diagnostics.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to provide and secure the service, answer and route business calls, produce transcripts and lead summaries, support users, improve reliability, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

3. AI and service providers

Calls are processed using OpenAI voice services. Telephony is provided through Twilio, and cloud hosting or email providers may process data on our behalf. These providers may process information outside Australia under their own security and privacy terms. We disclose only the information reasonably required to operate the service.

4. Caller notice and consent

At the start of every enabled call, the assistant identifies itself as AI and states that the call will be transcribed. Business customers must also ensure their use of NOTTLE AI and any additional notices comply with the laws that apply to their location, industry and callers.

5. Storage and retention

Account and call information is retained while the account is active or until the user deletes it. Users can delete individual calls, export account data or delete the complete account from Settings. Deleted information is removed from active application storage promptly. Residual copies held in infrastructure backups, if any, expire under the relevant provider’s backup cycle.

6. Security

We use access controls, password hashing, encrypted HTTPS connections, signed telephony requests and account separation. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should choose a unique password and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

7. Your choices and rights

You may access and correct business details in the app, export stored data, delete call records, or permanently delete your account. You may also contact us to request access, correction or deletion. We may need to verify identity before completing a request.

8. Children

NOTTLE AI is a business service and is not directed to children. Account holders must be at least 18 years old.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain available at this page.

10. Contact and complaints

Contact us first so we can investigate privacy questions or complaints. You may also have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
