How can we help?

Support for NOTTLE AI business accounts and callers.

Account and setup help

Email us with your account email, business name and a short description of the issue. Never send your password or OpenAI/Twilio credentials by email.

Phone number activation

New accounts show “Pending setup” until a compliant business number is assigned and tested. Number availability and identity requirements vary. We will confirm activation before you advertise or forward calls to the number.

Report a call problem

Include the approximate call date and time, the number called, and what went wrong. If the assistant interrupted, misunderstood details or had poor audio, this information helps us investigate without needing your password.

Privacy and data

You can export data, delete individual calls and delete your account in the app under Settings. You can also use our public account deletion instructions.

Response time

We aim to respond to support requests within two business days. NOTTLE AI support is not an emergency service.
